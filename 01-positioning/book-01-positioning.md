# Book 1 — Positioning Brief

## Working Title
**The Soft Life System**
### How to Automate Your Solopreneur Business to Work 15 Hours a Week

## Author
Deon ChristoLove

## One-Sentence Promise
In 90 days, you can redesign your solopreneur business so it runs on roughly 15 hours of focused work per week — without sacrificing revenue or the life you're building it for.

## The Big Idea
You don't need a bigger business. You need a lighter one.
The soft life is not a reward for hustling harder — it is a design problem. You don't need more discipline; you need better architecture.

## Target Reader
- **Primary:** Women solopreneurs, freelancers, coaches, consultants, and digital product creators, 28–55, earning $2k–$15k/month, burned out by a business that depends on their constant presence.
- **Secondary:** Professional women 40+ transitioning into entrepreneurship; side-hustlers who want to go full-time without recreating the job they left.
- **Psychographics:** Ambitious but exhausted. Values rest, autonomy, and quiet luxury. Rejects hustle culture but still wants to make real money. Has tried productivity hacks and is ready for systems.

## The Reader's Pain
- The business owns them. They can't take a day off without revenue dipping.
- They are the bottleneck for every task: sales, delivery, admin, marketing.
- They confuse being busy with being profitable.
- They feel guilty resting and can't enjoy the money they make.

## The Transformation
| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| 50–60 hour weeks | ~15 hours of focused work |
| Custom work for every client | A productized premium offer |
| Everything depends on you | Systems, templates, and automation |
| Guilt around rest | Rest as a business strategy |
| Revenue tied to hours | Revenue tied to leverage |

## Differentiation
Most "work less" books are written for men in tech or assume a team. This book is written for the solo woman building a real business — with warmth, boundaries, and a premium offer at the center. It combines the anti-hustle spirit of the soft life movement with the hard mechanics of automation.

## Signature Framework: The SOFT Method
- **S — Simplify:** Build the one-offer business.
- **O — Offer:** Package your genius into a premium, productized offer.
- **F — Fulfill:** Build the delivery machine.
- **T — Transfer:** Automate, template, and delegate until the business runs without you.

Then: **The 15-Hour Week Operating System** and the **90-Day Soft Life Launch Plan**.

## Voice & Tone
Warm, direct, confident, unhurried. Short paragraphs. Quotable lines. Practical, not preachy. "Soft" is strong, not fragile.
