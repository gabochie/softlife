# The Soft Life Series — Series Bible

This document keeps Books 1–5 consistent in voice, structure, design, and promise. Read it before outlining or drafting any new book in the series.

---

## 1. Series Promise

**The Soft Life Series** helps ambitious women build businesses that fund their lives without consuming them.

Every book delivers:

- a clear **system**, not a collection of tips;
- a **specific outcome** the reader can reach in 90 days or less;
- a **soft-life philosophy**: rest is strategy, leverage beats hustle, and enough is a design decision.

The series sits at the intersection of anti-hustle culture and hard business mechanics. It is warm in tone and rigorous in substance.

---

## 2. Reader

**Primary reader:** Women solopreneurs, freelancers, coaches, consultants, and digital product creators, ages 28–55, earning roughly $2k–$15k per month.

**Secondary reader:** Professional women 40+ moving into entrepreneurship; side-hustlers who want to go full-time without recreating the job they left.

**Psychographics:**

- Ambitious but exhausted.
- Values autonomy, rest, and quiet luxury.
- Rejects hustle culture but still wants to make real money.
- Has tried productivity hacks and is ready for architecture.

**The reader is not:** a venture-backed startup founder, a corporate executive, or someone looking for passive income with no work. The series is for women building real, small, profitable businesses.

## 3. Voice & Tone

### Voice rules

1. **Warm, direct, confident, unhurried.** Write like a smart friend who has done the work.
2. **Short paragraphs.** One idea per paragraph. White space is part of the design.
3. **Quotable lines.** Each chapter should contain at least one sentence the reader could screenshot.
4. **Practical, not preachy.** Name the problem, then give the mechanism.
5. **Soft is strong.** Never equate soft with fragile, lazy, or less ambitious.
6. **No shame.** The reader is not broken; her business design is.
7. **Specific over abstract.** Use numbers, scripts, checklists, and examples.

### Vocabulary

**Use:** soft life, leverage, architecture, operating system, premium offer, delivery machine, invisible team, quiet growth, enough, capacity, boundaries, maintenance.

**Avoid:** grind, hustle harder, crush it, scale fast, 10x, girlboss, passive income (unless qualified), six figures (as a promise), "just" (as in "just do this").

### Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Say "you built a job, not a business" | Say "you're doing it wrong" |
| Give one clear next step | Overwhelm with ten options |
| Use stories with named clients | Use vague "many entrepreneurs" |
| Show the math | Rely on inspiration alone |
| Name the trade-offs | Pretend everything is easy |

## 4. Structural System

Every book uses the same architecture:

- **5 parts**
- **15 chapters**
- **35,000–42,000 words**
- **Front matter:** title page, copyright, dedication, epigraph, contents, introduction, how to use this book
- **Back matter:** quick-start checklist, templates, scripts, worksheets, resources, about the author, also by, final note

### Chapter template

1. Opening story or scene
2. The core idea
3. Why it works / evidence
4. The framework or steps
5. A common mistake
6. **Your Soft Life Move** — action steps
7. **The Soft Life Summary** — 3–7 bullet recap

### Part structure

- **Part I:** Name the problem.
- **Part II:** Teach the core method.
- **Part III:** Build the operating system.
- **Part IV:** Protect it from the inside.
- **Part V:** Turn insight into a 90-day plan and a maintenance system.

## 6. Visual System

### Palette

The covers use a warm-to-cool gradient: terracotta, amber, and gold at the top, moving into deep plum, indigo, and violet at the bottom.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep background | Plum | `#2A0F4A` |
| Accent | Gold | `#D9B46A` |
| Light text | Cream | `#F5EAD9` |
| Warm highlight | Amber | `#FCAC03` |
| Cool highlight | Violet | `#5516C0` |

### Typography

- **Series and cover display:** Open Sans (800/700), tight tracking, uppercase for labels.
- **Interior body:** Open Sans 10.5 pt / 1.55 line height.
- **Chapter titles:** Open Sans 21 pt.
- **Part titles:** Open Sans 26 pt.
- **Running heads:** 8 pt uppercase, wide tracking.

If a serif is introduced later, use it for body text only and keep sans-serif for display.

### Cover composition

- Full-bleed gradient or illustration.
- Title in large, high-contrast type.
- Subtitle smaller, sentence case.
- Author name at the bottom.
- Series mark in a consistent position.
- Spine text reads top-to-bottom: title, separator, author.

### Interior design

- 6 × 9 in trim.
- Generous margins; 0.75 in inside, 0.6 in outside.
- Drop caps at chapter openings.
- Centred bullet ornament for section breaks.
- Checklists use open boxes (`□`).
- No decorative fonts, no clip art, no drop shadows.

## 7. Cross-Book Architecture

Each book must **stand alone** and **reward series readers**.

### Stand-alone rule

A reader who starts with Book 3 must understand every concept without reading Books 1–2. Reintroduce frameworks briefly when they appear.

### Series rewards

- A one-page "The Soft Life Series" map in the front or back matter.
- A consistent "Also by" page.
- A shared lead magnet and website.
- Cross-references that say "for the full money system, see *Soft Life Money*" rather than "as I said in Book 2."

### Shared assets

- Series website and email list.
- Series lead magnet: **The 15-Hour Week Quick-Start Checklist**.
- Series glossary (optional, in the back of each book).
- Series visual system and cover template.

## 9. Production Standards

- **Trim:** 6 × 9 in, 0.125 in bleed.
- **Interior:** black & white, white paper, 194 pages for Book 1.
- **Spine:** `page_count × 0.002252 in` for KDP white paper.
- **Cover wrap:** `12.25 + spine_width` × `9.25` in.
- **EPUB:** EPUB 3 with `nav.xhtml` and `toc.ncx`; one XHTML file per chapter.
- **Typography:** smart quotes, em dashes, en dashes, ellipses.
- **Placeholders:** all customization fields documented in `PLACEHOLDERS.md`.
- **File naming:** `the-soft-life-<slug>-print.pdf`, `the-soft-life-<slug>.epub`, `the-soft-life-<slug>-cover-wrap.pdf`.
- **Build:** `python3 06-production/build.py` for Book 1; replicate the script per book.

- [ ] The book follows the 5-part, 15-chapter structure.
- [ ] Every chapter ends with **Your Soft Life Move** and **The Soft Life Summary**.
- [ ] The voice matches the series rules.
- [ ] Recurring frameworks use the established names.
- [ ] The cover uses the series palette and typography.
- [ ] The interior uses the same trim, margins, and chapter template.
- [ ] Front and back matter include the series map and "Also by" page.
- [ ] All placeholders are replaced.
- [ ] EPUB, print PDF, and cover wrap are rebuilt and validated.
- [ ] The book stands alone but rewards series readers.
