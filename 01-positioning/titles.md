# High-Converting Book Titles

> **Rule:** Never sell "the soft life." Sell the **system, tool, or outcome** that builds it.
> Format: `Soft Life + Functional Utility + Specific Outcome`

---

## The Title Formula

A high-converting Soft Life title needs four ingredients:

1. **Hook** — "Soft Life" (the aspirational keyword)
2. **Utility** — the concrete mechanism (system, blueprint, budget, reset, method)
3. **Outcome** — the measurable result (15-hour week, calm wealth, no burnout)
4. **Audience / Timeframe** — who it's for or how fast (for women, in 30 days, without going broke)

Example: **The Soft Life System: Automate Your Solopreneur Business to Work 15 Hours a Week**

## Title Testing Checklist

Before locking a title, confirm:

- [ ] Does it name a **specific mechanism** (system, blueprint, method, reset)?
- [ ] Does it promise a **measurable or vivid outcome**?
- [ ] Does it pass the **"does this sound like a system or a vibe?"** test?
- [ ] Would it stand out in a saturated #softlife feed?
- [ ] Does the subtitle do the selling even if the title is generic?
- [ ] Is it searchable and shareable in under 60 characters (main title)?
