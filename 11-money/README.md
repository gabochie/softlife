## Quick verdict

The product is commercially viable, but it is **not launch-ready**. The current
$19.99 bundle is too cheap to combine with a 50% affiliate rate and paid ads.
Test at **$19.99 founding**, keep affiliates at **30%**, then move to
**$29.99 regular** once proof and conversion are established.

| Scenario | Gross | Net after fees/refunds/affiliate | Profit after fixed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 direct buyers at $19.99 | $1,999 | $1,649 | $149 |
| 1,000 direct buyers at $19.99 | $19,990 | $16,492 | $14,992 |
| 1,000 buyers, 30% affiliate on 30% | $19,990 | $14,692 | $13,192 |
| 1,000 buyers, 50% affiliate on all | $19,990 | $6,497 | $4,997 |

## Key assumptions

- Selar USD fee: **10% + $0.50** per sale.
- Refund reserve: **5%**.
- Affiliate commission: 30% or 50%, as stated per row.
- Fixed costs: **$1,500** in the base case.
- Excludes tax, owner time and content production.
