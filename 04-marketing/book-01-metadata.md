## Audience

**Primary reader:** Women solopreneurs, coaches, consultants, and service providers, ages 28–45, earning $3k–$15k/month, who are successful but exhausted.

**Secondary reader:** Anyone transitioning from employment to self-employment who wants to build a business that does not consume their life.

**Reader pain points:**
- Working more than 40 hours a week with no end in sight
- Revenue tied directly to hours worked
- Constant context switching and decision fatigue
- Guilt around rest and boundaries
- Too many offers, too many clients, too much complexity

**Reader desired outcomes:**
- A clear, premium offer that attracts the right clients
- A business that runs on systems, not willpower
- A 15-hour workweek that protects their life
- Boundaries that hold without guilt
- Sustainable income without burnout

## Positioning

**The promise:** Build a solopreneur business that runs on systems — and work 15 hours a week.

**The differentiator:** Most business books teach you to grow faster. This one teaches you to build lighter. It combines offer design, delivery systems, automation, and mindset into one integrated method (the SOFT Method) designed specifically for solopreneurs who want freedom, not just revenue.

**The hook:** You don't have a business. You have a job with extra steps. This book shows you how to fix that.

## Comparable Titles

- *The 4-Hour Workweek* by Timothy Ferriss
- *Company of One* by Paul Jarvis
- *Buy Back Your Time* by Dan Martell
- *The E-Myth Revisited* by Michael E. Gerber
- *Profit First* by Mike Michalowicz (for the systems mindset)

**How this book is different:** It is written specifically for women solopreneurs, centers the soft life philosophy over hustle, and provides a complete operating system (SOFT Method + 15-Hour Week + 90-Day Plan) rather than a collection of tactics.
