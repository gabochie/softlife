# The Soft Life System — Affiliate Resource Guide

Monetization plan for the tools and books recommended to readers. The curated resource
list lives on **studio.gabochie.com** — every link below should be registered to you, then
placed on that page and in your emails/social posts. **Never put affiliate links inside the
book itself** (Amazon KDP prohibits affiliate links in Kindle books, and in-book links can't
be updated after print runs anyway).

Prepared Nov 11, 2026. Terms verified where marked ✅ (from official pages or multiple
recent directories); unverified items marked ⚠️ — confirm on the program page before
relying on the numbers.

---

## How to use this guide

1. Register for the **Tier 1** programs first (best fit + easiest approval). Approval can take
   days to weeks, so start now.
2. Build one page on studio.gabochie.com: "The Soft Life Stack — tools I actually use,"
   with your affiliate links and a clear disclosure at the top.
3. Each program pays through a different network. You'll need accounts on
   **PartnerStack, Impact, Partnerize, CJ/Awin, and Dub** — one signup each, then apply to
   individual programs from inside.
4. Disclose on every page/post that contains a link (see disclosure templates at the end).

## Tier 1 — Register first (best fit, easy approval)

### Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — email marketing ✅
- **Commission:** 50% of each referred customer's first 12 months; 10–20% recurring
  indefinitely after that if you refer 10+ paying customers/year (Bronze/Silver/Gold tiers)
- **Cookie:** 90 days · **Network:** PartnerStack · **Min. payout:** $5 (PayPal/Stripe)
- **Apply:** kit.com/affiliates
- **Why:** The book's "Communicate" role. Creator-focused, matches the reader perfectly.
  ~$175–200 per conversion on the entry paid plan.

### systeme.io — all-in-one funnels/email/courses ✅
- **Commission:** 60% lifetime recurring, no application, no cap, free plan for referrals
- **Cookie:** lifetime attribution · **Network:** in-house · **Payouts:** from $30
- **Apply:** systeme.io/affiliate-program
- **Why:** All-in-one budget tool (funnels + email + courses + automation). Very popular
  with African solopreneurs; strong fit for the book's "start simple" message. ~$10/mo
  recurring per referral at entry pricing — compounds over time.

### Pabbly Connect — automation ✅
- **Commission:** 30% recurring for the lifetime of the customer (20% flat on lifetime deals)
- **Cookie:** 365 days · **Network:** in-house · **Min. payout:** $50 (PayPal / INR bank)
- **Apply:** pabbly.com/affiliates
- **Why:** The book's "automation engine" role at a budget price. Longest cookie in the
  category; open sign-up; India-based so INR payouts work. No paid-search ads allowed.

### Make.com — automation ✅
- **Commission:** 35% recurring for 12 months
- **Cookie:** not published · **Network:** in-house · **Approval:** open sign-up
- **Why:** The premium visual automation tool readers will graduate to from Pabbly.

### FreshBooks — invoicing & accounting ✅
- **Commission:** up to $200 per paying customer, up to $10 per free trial (starts ~$55
  per sale, scales with volume)
- **Cookie:** 120 days · **Network:** PartnerStack · **Approval:** manual review (they want
  original content aimed at freelancers/service businesses — this book qualifies)
- **Apply:** freshbooks.com/affiliate-program
- **Why:** The book's "Get paid" role. One-time but high-value; 120-day cookie is the
  longest in finance software.

### ClickUp — projects & delivery ✅
- **Commission:** up to $25 per free workspace signup (tiered perks at 100/200 signups/mo)
- **Cookie:** 30 days · **Network:** in-house (clickup.com/affiliates) · **Approval:** easy
- **Why:** The book's "Deliver" role. Free-signup bounty converts well from tutorial content.

### Dubsado — client management ✅
- **Commission:** $35 cash per referral who subscribes and stays 60 days; invite tiers
  reach $50/$75 · referral gets 20% off first month/year
- **Network:** Dub (their new partner platform, launched Oct 2026)
- **Apply:** app.dubsado.com/affiliate (needs a free Dubsado account)
- **Why:** The "Capture → Deliver → Get paid" pipeline for service solopreneurs.

### Canva — design ✅
- **Commission:** up to $36 per new paid-plan signup
- **Network:** in-house (Canva Partners / "Canvassador") · confirm current terms at
  canva.com before relying on the figure
- **Why:** Readers will build their brand assets, lead magnets, and templates here.

### Buffer — social media scheduling ✅
- **Commission:** 25% recurring for 12 months per referred customer
- **Cookie:** 90 days · **Network:** Dub (partners.dub.co/buffer/apply) · free to join,
  manual review (3–5 business days) · **Payout:** monthly via Dub
- **Why:** Buffer is already named in the book's social media workflow — this turns
  that mention into income. Entry plans are $5/mo/channel, so it converts easily on
  creator-focused content.

## Africa / Ghana stack — verified programs

These are the picks to feature for readers in Ghana and across Africa. Several also pay
through methods that work without PayPal (a real constraint for African affiliates).

### Selar — sell digital products (Africa's creator commerce platform) ✅
- **Refer & Earn:** 10% commission on every subscription payment a referred creator makes
  (recurring, no cap) + 20% annual commission if they join Selar's affiliate network;
  referred user gets 5% off. Requires your own active paid Selar/Affiliate plan.
- **Affiliate Network:** a separate earner — promote 30,000+ African digital products for
  up to 70% per sale (NGN 3,000/year membership)
- **Network:** in-house · **Payouts:** instant to Selar wallet, no minimum; supports
  Ghana cedis, NGN, USD, and mobile-money/bank payouts
- **Apply:** selar.com (Refer & Earn is in your dashboard) · affiliates.selar.com (network)
- **Why:** The African Gumroad. Readers use it to sell ebooks/courses/services with cards,
  bank transfer, and mobile money. **Double opportunity for you:** list The Soft Life
  System course/templates on Selar AND recruit affiliates to sell them — you earn from
  your own product sales while your referral links earn from their subscriptions.

### Payoneer — get paid by international clients ✅
- **Commission:** ~$25 per new customer who becomes active (directory-cited figure;
  Payoneer does not publish the rate — confirm at onboarding)
- **Cookie:** 30 days · **Network:** PartnerStack / in-house · **Payouts:** monthly to
  your Payoneer balance or bank
- **Apply:** payoneer.com/affiliate-program
- **Why:** The standard for African freelancers receiving USD/GBP/EUR from global
  platforms and clients; withdraws to Ghanaian banks in GHS. A Wise alternative for
  readers without the documents Wise needs.

### Fiverr — get clients / get work ✅
- **Commission:** 25% of a new buyer's first order + 10% revenue share for 12 months;
  Fiverr Pro 70% of first order; Logo Maker $30 fixed
- **Network:** in-house (fiverr.com/partnerships/affiliates) · **Min. payout:** $100,
  net-30 to bank (no PayPal required)
- **Why:** Two-sided relevance: readers who need their first clients AND readers who sell
  services. Strong conversions from "how to start freelancing" content aimed at Africa.

### Grey — virtual USD/GBP/EUR accounts ✅
- **Commission:** $5 per referred user (both sides) after the referral deposits $500
- **Network:** in-app "Affiliate & Referrals" — instant, no application
- **Why:** Works in Ghana: GHS withdrawals, virtual dollar cards for paying for SaaS
  subscriptions (the single biggest blocker for African readers buying tools). Small
  bounty, but zero friction — every Ghanaian reader can promote it from day one.

### Raenest (formerly Geegpay) — virtual dollar accounts ⚠️
- **Commission:** $5 per successful referral (friend gets $2), capped at $152 total
- **Network:** in-app referral · **Verify** current terms before featuring
- **Why:** Nigeria/Kenya-focused alternative to Grey — feature for Nigerian readers.

### Zoho — business suite (CRM, Books, Invoice, Forms) ✅
- **Commission:** 15% of first-12-months revenue per qualified sale (18%/20% at
  Super/Elite tiers) · **Cookie:** 90 days · **Min. payout:** $100 (PayPal or wire)
- **Caveat:** the referral must sign up in the same data center region you registered in
  — register in the DC matching your audience; commission is on the first product only
- **Apply:** zoho.com/affiliate · referred customers get $100 wallet credit
- **Why:** The affordable alternative to the whole Western stack: Zoho Invoice is free,
  Books handles accounting, Campaigns is email — all accepted across African markets.

### Hostinger — websites & hosting ✅
- **Commission:** up to 40% on qualifying 12-month+ initial purchases (AI Builder up to
  60% via special offers) · **Cookie:** 30 days · **Cap:** $300/sale
- **Network:** in-house + CJ · **Apply:** hostinger.com/affiliates or via CJ
- **Why:** Cheapest credible hosting + domain for a Ghanaian solopreneur's first website;
  pays out internationally without PayPal problems.

## The books (Further Reading section) — Amazon + Bookshop.org

The "Resources & Further Reading" list in the back matter names 12 books. Monetize it:

### Amazon Associates
- **Apply:** affiliate-program.amazon.com · Books pay ~4.5% (varies by category)
- **Rules:** 24-hour cookie, strict disclosure + no link-spamming, must make 3 sales in
  180 days to keep the account
- **Use:** a single "See the full reading list" link on studio.gabochie.com (a storefront
  list of all 12 books) rather than links inside the book

### Bookshop.org ✅
- **Commission:** 10% per book (up to 15% enhanced program) · **Attribution:** 48 hours
- **Apply:** bookshop.org/affiliates/profile
- **Why:** Ethical angle fits the brand ("support independent bookstores"), and you can
  build a public storefront list of the 12 recommended books — link it from your site
  and your author email signature.

## Disclosure templates

Website resource page (required by FTC; add at top of studio.gabochie.com/stack):

> Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you sign up through them, I earn a
> commission at no extra cost to you. I only recommend tools I believe are worth your
> money — several are here even though they pay me nothing.

Email/social footer:

> Disclaimer: links to tools may be affiliate links. Your price never changes.

KDP note: do **not** add these links or disclosures inside the ebook/print book —
Amazon's Associates policy and KDP terms prohibit affiliate links in Kindle content.
Keep the book pointing to studio.gabochie.com only.
