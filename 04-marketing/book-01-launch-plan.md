# Book 1 — Launch Plan

**Book:** *The Soft Life System*
**Subtitle:** How to Automate Your Solopreneur Business to Work 15 Hours a Week
**Launch model:** 12-week pre-launch, 1 launch week, 90 days of post-launch optimisation

---

## 1. Launch Goal

### Primary goal

Sell **1,000 copies** in the first 90 days and collect **50 honest reviews**.

### Supporting goals

- Build the series email list to **500 subscribers**.
- Land **5 podcast or guest-post appearances**.
- Establish a repeatable evergreen funnel that sells the book without daily effort.
- Validate the positioning and messaging for Books 2–5.

### Success metrics

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Launch-week sales | 300–500 |
| 90-day sales | 1,000 |
| Reviews in 90 days | 50 |
| Email subscribers | 500 |
| Amazon category rank | Top 20 in two categories |
| Ad spend | ≤ 30% of launch revenue |
| Refund rate | < 5% |

---

## 2. Positioning for Launch

**Hook:** *You don't have a business. You have a job with extra steps.*

**Promise:** *Build a solopreneur business that runs on systems — and work 15 hours a week.*

**Proof points:**

- The SOFT Method: Simplify, Offer, Fulfill, Transfer.
- The 15-Hour Week Operating System.
- The 90-Day Soft Life Launch Plan.
- Templates, scripts, and worksheets in the back of the book.

**Primary message:** This is not another productivity book. It is an operating system for women who want a lighter business and a fuller life.

## 5. Pricing & Promotion

| Format | Launch price | Regular price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ebook | $0.99 (launch week) | $9.99 |
| Paperback | $19.99 | $19.99 |
| Hardcover | — | $29.99 (optional) |
| Audiobook | TBD | TBD |

**Strategy:**

- Use the $0.99 launch price to drive rank, reviews, and visibility.
- Do not discount the paperback; it anchors the value.
- Offer a free chapter and the quick-start checklist as lead magnets.
- Consider a **KDP Select** 90-day enrollment for launch to maximise page reads and reviews. Trade-off: exclusivity. If the author has an audience on other retailers, go wide instead.
- Run a **Countdown Deal** or category-specific promotion in weeks 5–8 if sales plateau.

## 6. Launch Team & ARC Readers

### Recruit

- Email list subscribers who have engaged in the last 90 days.
- Social followers who have commented or shared.
- Communities the author is already part of (not drive-by promotion).
- 10 personal invitations to people who will genuinely review.

### ARC process

1. Send a personal email with the ARC and a one-line ask.
2. Give a clear review deadline: **two weeks before launch**.
3. Provide three review prompts:
   - What problem did the book solve for you?
   - Which framework or chapter was most useful?
   - Who would you recommend it to?
4. Send one reminder five days before the deadline.
5. Thank every reviewer personally.

### Review request script

> Hi [NAME],
>
> Thank you for reading *The Soft Life System*. If the book helped you, would you be willing to leave an honest review on Amazon? Even two sentences make a real difference for a new book.
>
> Here's the link: [REVIEW LINK]
>
> Thank you for being part of this launch.
>
> Deon ChristoLove

## 9. Ads

### Amazon Ads

- Start with **exact-match** keywords from `book-01-metadata.md`.
- Budget: $10–15 per day.
- Target ACOS: 40–60% during launch, 30% after.
- Pause keywords with no sales after 1,000 impressions.
- Add competitor titles as product targets after the first two weeks.

### Meta Ads

- Budget: $10–15 per day.
- Audience: women 28–55, business owners, coaches, consultants, freelancers.
- Creative: book cover + hook + testimonial.
- Landing page: book page with email capture.
- Kill any ad above $2 cost per click after 3 days.

### Tracking

- UTM-tag every link.
- Track sales, email signups, and cost per acquisition.
- Review ads weekly; do not optimise daily.

## 10. Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Low review count | Extend ARC outreach; send personal review requests; do not buy reviews. |
| Sales plateau | Run a limited-time promotion; pitch more podcasts; refresh ad creative. |
| Ad costs too high | Pause underperforming ads; shift budget to email and organic. |
| Launch fatigue | Batch content; reuse frameworks; protect one no-work day per week. |
| Negative review | Respond once, politely; use feedback to improve the next edition. |
| KDP rejection | Check trim, bleed, spine, and barcode area; rebuild and resubmit. |

---

## 11. Post-Launch Evergreen Funnel

1. Social post or podcast → free chapter page.
2. Free chapter page → email signup.
3. Welcome email → quick-start checklist.
4. Nurture sequence → book offer.
5. Book buyers → review request → series readers.

The goal is a funnel that sells the book without another launch.
