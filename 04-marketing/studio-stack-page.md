# The Soft Life Stack

Tools that run the six systems from *The Soft Life System* — the same roles you mapped
in Chapter 9: Capture, Communicate, Schedule, Deliver, Get paid, Analyze.

You don't need all of these. You need the simplest tool that does the job and that
you'll actually use. Start with the free or entry plan of one tool per system, and add
more only when a real problem shows up.

## 🌍 Africa / Ghana — start here

### Get paid — first, because cash flow is everything

**[Selar](LINK)** — Sell your ebooks, courses, services, and tickets; customers pay with
cards, bank transfer, and mobile money, and you withdraw in cedis. Free plan to start.
→ My pick for turning your knowledge into income — local payments, no stress.

**[Payoneer](LINK)** — Receive USD, GBP, and EUR from international clients and
platforms, withdraw straight to your Ghanaian bank. 
→ My pick for anyone working with foreign clients.

**[Grey](LINK)** — Virtual US/UK/EU bank accounts with a dollar card for paying for the
tools below, plus GHS withdrawals. Get $5 when you fund your account through this link.
→ My pick for paying for international subscriptions from Ghana.

**[Wise](LINK)** — The global standard for cross-border payments; works alongside
Payoneer/Grey for receiving and sending internationally.
→ My pick for sending and receiving across currencies.

### Get clients — and get hired

**[Fiverr](LINK)** — The world's freelance marketplace. Use it to land your first
clients, hire help as you grow, or sell your services globally.
→ My pick for the fastest path from skills to income.

### Run the six systems

**[systeme.io](LINK)** — Forms, CRM, email, funnels, and courses in one dashboard.
Free plan, paid from ~$10/mo.
→ My pick for one tool instead of five — the whole Capture + Communicate system.

**[Zoho](LINK)** — A full business suite (CRM, invoicing, books, email) at African-friendly
prices. Zoho Invoice is free forever. $100 in wallet credits via this link.
→ My pick when you want serious software without serious prices.

**[Cal.com](LINK)** — Let clients book themselves; generous free tier.
→ My pick for scheduling without another subscription.

**[ClickUp](LINK)** — Projects, docs, and client workspaces. The free plan is genuinely
enough for a solo business.
→ My pick for keeping client work organized.

**[Pabbly Connect](LINK)** — Connect your tools ("form filled → add to email list →
create task") at a price that makes sense here.
→ My pick to start automating cheaply.

**[Canva](LINK)** — Brand assets, lead magnets, social graphics. Free plan; Pro unlocks
the brand kit.
→ My pick for looking professional on day one.

**[Buffer](LINK)** — Schedule a week of social posts in one sitting. Free plan; paid
from $5/mo/channel.
→ My pick for consistency without doom-scrolling.

**[Hostinger](LINK)** — Your first website + domain for the price of lunch, with
WordPress or their AI builder.
→ My pick for a credible online home.

### The Ghana minimum viable stack
1. **Selar** (free) — capture clients and get paid, local or international
2. **Cal.com** (free) — let clients book themselves
3. **systeme.io** (free) — email your list and sell
