# The 24 careers

Each profile follows the same structure so you can compare without being swayed
by a good story. The numbers are national. Your city, seniority, and contract
will change the outcome. The editorial scores tell you how the role tends to
feel; they do not tell you how your specific employer will behave.

Read the data block first. Then read “The soft version” and “The catch.” If the
catch is a dealbreaker, move on. If the soft version describes the life you
want, put the role on your shortlist and test it.

All pay figures are annual medians from BLS OEWS May 2025. The percentile range
is the 10th to 90th percentile. Growth and openings are BLS Employment
Projections 2025–35. The Soft Life Score uses the formula in Chapter 2.
