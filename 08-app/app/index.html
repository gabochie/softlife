Questions people ask

What is The Soft Life System?

The Soft Life System is a book and app for women who want to build a business that fits their life. It focuses on one premium offer, a repeatable delivery machine, clear boundaries and a 90-day launch plan.

Is the app free?

Yes. The free tier includes the introduction and Chapter 1 audio, the 90-day task plan, progress tracking and the affiliate link. The Complete bundle unlocks all 15 chapters and worksheets inside the app.

What does the Complete bundle include?

The Complete bundle is $19.99 at the founding price (regular $29.99). It includes the full 3.8-hour read-aloud, the written chapters, the 90-day plan, worksheets and the monthly review ritual — all inside your existing free account.

How does the 15-hour week work?

The week is designed around four jobs: genius work, growth, delivery and operations, and margin. A simple split is four hours of genius work, four hours of growth, four hours of delivery and three hours of review and rest.

Do I need a team to work 15 hours a week?

No. Start with one repeatable offer, templates and a protected calendar. A team comes later, when the process is visible enough to hand over.
