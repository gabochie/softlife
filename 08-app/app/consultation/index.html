Questions before you apply

Is this coaching or consulting?

It is a one-to-one business audit. We review your business, make decisions and produce a written 90-day plan. It is not ongoing coaching or done-for-you delivery.

What if I do not have a business yet?

The audit works best when you already have an offer, a skill you sell or an early business to examine. If you are still at the idea stage, start with the Complete bundle and the free app first.

How do I pay?

After we confirm fit, I send a secure payment link and booking options. Your spot is confirmed when payment is received.

What if we are not a fit?

I will tell you before you pay. If the audit is not the right next step, I will point you to the product or resource that fits better.

Is the fee refundable?

Yes, under the written-plan guarantee above. Read the full refund policy for the general terms.
