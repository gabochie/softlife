How it works: the four jobs

Every week has four jobs. The mistake is trying to do all four every day.

1 · Choose

Decide who you serve

One audience, one outcome, and what you will not do. Everything else is a distraction.

2 · Create

Build the offer and experience

Make the offer, the content, and the client experience that deliver the outcome.

3 · Carry

Build the systems that carry routine work

Templates, automations, saved replies — the "business brain" that runs without you.

4 · Check

Review, adjust, protect the margin

Review the numbers, the calendar, and the energy. Then adjust.

When you know which job you're doing, you stop confusing motion with progress. A 15-hour week is simply a week where each job gets its proper share.
